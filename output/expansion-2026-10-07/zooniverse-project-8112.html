    About Heritage Quest

    Help archaeologists discover traces from our past on high-resolution elevation maps created using lasers mounted on aircrafts (LiDAR). These laser beams pass through vegetation present, and as a result, thousands of undiscovered prehistoric burial mounds, ancient roads and other features previously hidden, lie waiting to be discovered…
