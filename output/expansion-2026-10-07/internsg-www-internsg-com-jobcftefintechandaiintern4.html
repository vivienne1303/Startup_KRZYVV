Company Profile

At CFTE, we work with governments, central banks, financial institutions and technology companies to help them prepare for an AI-driven future. And we apply the same transformation mindset to our own team. Our goal? To build a team of Supercharged Professionals – individuals who combine: Domain expertise, Future-proof skills, The ability to leverage AI through and through. This is for those who want to achieve exceptional results, and make an impact on millions.

Job Description

We’re hiring Fintech and AI Summer Interns – someone who takes ownership, creates structure from ambiguity, and gets things done.

In this role, you’ll support high-impact initiatives across AI, digital transformation, and future skills. You’ll work with global teams, manage project workflows, and use AI tools daily to drive efficiency and insight.

If you learn fast, adapt quickly, and want to grow into a systems thinker, this is for you.

What You’ll Do

  • Break down complex projects into actionable plans and timelines
  • Coordinate across teams to ensure clear execution and delivery
  • Use AI tools (ChatGPT, Notion AI, Copilot, etc.) to boost productivity
  • Communicate clearly and proactively with internal and external stakeholders
  • Spot problems early, propose solutions, and keep momentum high
  • Reflect, learn, and improve every week

 

Here’s what we look for:

  • A sense of ownership - you follow through, double check, and don’t let things slip
  • An entrepreneurial mindset - you find a way, even if the path isn’t clear
  • Comfort with complexity and speed - you structure your thoughts and act decisively
  • High digital fluency - ideally, you’re already using AI tools at an advanced level, from Custom GPTs to Agents and Vibe Coding. 
  • Above all, intensity and curiosity - we want people who learn fast and care deeply
