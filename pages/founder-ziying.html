In my own words

What does TeenLaunch mean to you?

To me, TeenLaunch is more than just a platform — it is a commitment to helping young people discover what they are capable of. I believe that every student deserves the opportunity to explore their interests, develop their potential, and take their first steps towards their aspirations. Through TeenLaunch, I hope to bridge the gap between young people and the opportunities available to them, so that no one has to miss out simply because they did not know where to look. Ultimately, TeenLaunch represents our belief that young people should not have to wait for opportunities to come to them; they should be empowered to seek them out and create their own.

— Zhuo Ziying
