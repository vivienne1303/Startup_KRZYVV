01 / My story

A little about me

I am an Information Technology student at Singapore Polytechnic, specialising in UX/UI Design, and a Product Engineer Intern at X Corp Edutech. My experience includes website and app development, user interface design, youth engagement and event planning. As a co-founder of TeenLaunch, I help turn our ideas into a platform that supports young people in discovering opportunities and building their portfolios.

02 / Looking ahead

What I'm working towards

Right now

Present aspirations

I want to make TeenLaunch useful and easy to use, helping youths explore their interests, gain experience and feel more confident about their next steps. Along the way, I hope to strengthen my skills in product development, design and leadership.

The next chapter

Future aspirations

I aspire to start my own business beyond TeenLaunch, combining my interests in technology, design and entrepreneurship. Through building TeenLaunch, I hope to gain the experience and confidence to turn new ideas into useful products and grow as a business owner.

In my own words

What does TeenLaunch mean to you?

To me, TeenLaunch represents the courage to start, even when you do not have everything figured out. As a young person myself, I understand how difficult it can be to find opportunities and decide what comes next. TeenLaunch is my way of helping other youths take that first step while learning and growing alongside them.

— Vivienne Lau Xuan Ying
