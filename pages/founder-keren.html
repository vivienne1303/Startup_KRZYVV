01 / My story

A little about me

I am a student leader and aspiring entrepreneur who is interested in turning ideas into initiatives that create tangible impact. I am particularly drawn to identifying gaps in existing systems and exploring how technology can offer practical solutions to real-world challenges. Through TeenLaunch, I seek to address a challenge faced by many young people: navigating an overwhelming landscape of opportunities without a clear starting point. My approach centres on building a solution that is not only innovative, but also practical, accessible, and capable of creating lasting value.

02 / Looking ahead

What I'm working towards

Right now

Present aspirations

As CEO of TeenLaunch, I lead the team in turning our vision into reality, guiding the platform's direction and working towards making it a sustainable initiative. I hope to grow TeenLaunch into a trusted platform where young people can discover opportunities that match their interests and take meaningful steps towards their goals.

The next chapter

Future aspirations

Beyond the platform itself, I want TeenLaunch to bring youths and organisations closer together, creating more ways for young people to learn, gain experience, and contribute to society. In the long run, I hope TeenLaunch can help build a culture where young people feel empowered to pursue their ambitions instead of feeling limited by what they already know or have access to.

In my own words

What does TeenLaunch mean to you?

To me, TeenLaunch represents the belief that a young person's future should not be limited by what they happen to know or who they happen to know. There are countless opportunities for young people to learn, grow, and discover their potential, yet many remain out of reach simply because they do not know where to find them. I want TeenLaunch to change that. Beyond helping youths discover opportunities, I hope we can empower them to take initiative, gain meaningful experiences, and shape their own paths. Ultimately, TeenLaunch is more than a platform to me; it is a step towards a society where young people are not merely waiting for opportunities, but are equipped to recognise, pursue, and create them.

— Zong Keren
